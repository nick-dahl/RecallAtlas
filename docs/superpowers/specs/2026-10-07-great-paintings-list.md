**For:** review and editing before any image is fetched (spec §3.1, `2026-10-07-great-paintings-design.md`).
**Count:** 246 paintings — 213 Western, 33 from other traditions and the ancient world; 8 anonymous.

**How to edit:** strike or delete a row to cut it, add a row to include a work, or change a movement or rank. I will re-run the checks (at most 4 per artist, at least 6 per movement, at most 8 anonymous, nothing after 1930) on your edits.

**Rank** is the order paintings are introduced: 1–50 are hand-ranked; after that, very famous works come before canonical ones, alternating between movements so no session gets stuck in one century. **Also** lists boundary movements that are never offered as wrong answers.

## Considered and left out

- **Picasso and Braque.** Pre-1931 works are public domain in the US, but both are still under copyright in France and Spain, so Wikimedia Commons does not host them and no museum releases them openly. Cubism is carried by Juan Gris, Léger, Delaunay and Gleizes instead.
- **Still in copyright everywhere we could source from:** Balla, Severini, Duchamp, Kokoschka, Nolde, Chagall, Kupka, Goncharova, Vlaminck — and everything after 1930 (Dalí, Kahlo, Hopper, O'Keeffe, Magritte, Pollock, Rothko, Warhol).
- **Anonymous works over the cap of 8:** the *Hamzanama*, the *Tale of Genji* scrolls, the *Codex Borgia*, Ravenna and Byzantine mosaics (mosaic, not painting).
- **Prints outside ukiyo-e:** Dürer's *Melencolia I*, Goya's *The Sleep of Reason*, Toulouse-Lautrec and Mucha posters.
- **Cut for balance (easy to swap back in):** Botticelli *Venus and Mars*, Gozzoli *Procession of the Magi*, Perugino *Delivery of the Keys*, van Eyck *Madonna of Chancellor Rolin*, Matsys *The Moneylender and His Wife*, Bellini *Doge Leonardo Loredan*, El Greco *Opening of the Fifth Seal*, Velázquez *The Surrender of Breda*, Jan Steen *The Merry Family*, Hobbema *The Avenue at Middelharnis*, Tiepolo's Würzburg ceiling, Ingres *Monsieur Bertin* and *The Turkish Bath*, Turner *The Slave Ship*, Friedrich *Monk by the Sea*, Goya *The Family of Charles IV*, Repin *Ivan the Terrible and His Son*, Waterhouse *Hylas and the Nymphs*, Boccioni *States of Mind*, Mondrian *The Gray Tree*, Klee *Twittering Machine*.
- **Possible additions from India:** Raja Ravi Varma (*Shakuntala*, 1870s) and Abanindranath Tagore (*Bharat Mata*, 1905). Both are public domain but would need a movement label we don't have (Academic art, Bengal School).
